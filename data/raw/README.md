# Local document corpus

Place PDF or HTML guidelines here for local ingestion. Documents, derived medical
data and uploaded files are not part of the source-code distribution.

Use source documents that you are permitted to process. Preserve their source URL,
title, version and publication date when preparing an evaluation corpus.

Source portals used by the project include:

- Russian clinical recommendations: https://cr.minzdrav.gov.ru/
- European Society of Cardiology guidelines: https://www.escardio.org/Guidelines
- American Heart Association journals: https://www.ahajournals.org/
- NICE guidelines: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance

These links identify source publishers; they do not grant redistribution rights or
pin a particular document version. Download suitable documents from the publisher.
